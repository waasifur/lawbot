# Phase 4 GRPO evaluation

Pending Colab/Kaggle GPU run. `notebooks/reward_model_lawbot.ipynb` will write pre/post validation loss and perplexity, ROUGE-L, BERTScore F1, exact citation accuracy, and source-wise ROUGE-L here.

The experiment uses only 40 answerable prompts, eight synthetic unanswerable prompts, and four heuristic rewards. This is an exploratory prototype; its rewards and metrics do not establish legal correctness or safety.
